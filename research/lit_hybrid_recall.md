# Literature: better in-context recall for linear-recurrent / hybrid LMs, vs. ICE (26 Oct 2026)

All arXiv ids below were opened and the title/authors/date confirmed. Items marked [not opened] were not verified.
Search caveat: web search found NO paper that combines (exact token-ID n-gram match) + (hybrid GDN/Mamba LM) + (trained input/output injection). That is mildly reassuring for novelty but the search is not exhaustive.

## A. Recall gap in efficient LMs (diagnosis + theory)
1. **Zoology: Measuring and Improving Recall in Efficient Language Models** - Arora, Eyuboglu, Timalsina, Johnson, Poli, Zou, Rudra, Re; Dec 2023; arXiv:2312.04927. Most of the perplexity gap of gated-conv models to attention comes from associative recall (MQAR); hybrids with *input-dependent sparse attention* close 97.4% of the gap. ICE relation: ICE is an extreme "input-dependent sparse" mechanism (exact match instead of learned); use MQAR-style synthetic as a sanity test and cite as the origin of the recall-gap framing.
2. **Simple linear attention LMs balance the recall-throughput tradeoff (BASED)** - Arora et al.; Feb 2024; arXiv:2402.18668. State size vs recall is a tradeoff curve; linear attn + sliding window is the Pareto point. ICE relation: ICE adds a different axis (41 B/token outside the model); plot ICE on a state-size/bytes-per-token vs recall curve like BASED.
3. **Repeat After Me: Transformers are Better than SSMs at Copying** - Jelassi, Brandfonbrener, Kakade, Malach; Feb 2024; arXiv:2402.01032. Fixed-state models provably cannot copy long strings; transformers can. ICE relation: this is the theory motivating ICE; ICE sidesteps the state bound with an external table (copy is exactly what n-gram lookup does).
4. **Just Read Twice (JRT)** - Arora, Timalsina, Singhal, Spector, Eyuboglu, Zhao, Rao, Rudra, Re; Jul 2024; arXiv:2407.05483. Recall difficulty ~ set disjointness, order-dependent; repeating the context (JRT-Prompt, +11 pts) or non-causal prefix linear attention (JRT-RNN) closes most of the gap. ICE relation: JRT needs 2x prefill and is not applicable to generated tokens; a compute-cheap baseline to compare against at prefill; ICE is causal and works at decode too.
5. **On the Recall Scaling Laws in Mamba (via hashing)** - Koren, Ben-Kish, Giryes, Wolf, Zimerman; Sep 2026; arXiv:2609.07681. Mamba recalls by implicitly learning linear hash functions; capacity scales with state dim vs (vocab x #facts). ICE relation: gives a theoretical reason why exact table lookup helps: ICE removes hash collisions of the learned state; can frame ICE as "exact hash for the key->successor relation".
6. **In-Context Language Learning: Architectures and Algorithms** - Akyurek, Wang, Kim, Andreas; Jan 2024; arXiv:2401.12973. Transformers use "n-gram heads" (higher-order induction heads); hard-wiring n-gram heads into other models lowers perplexity up to 6.7% at 340M. **Closest prior art to ICE** (hard-wired n-gram heads, but implemented as attention over the sequence, not a hash table with trust buckets). ICE relation: must be cited and distinguished (ICE: CPU hash table, no KV, constant bytes/token, trust signals, output logit bonus).

## B. Better recurrent states (alternatives / orthogonal to ICE)
7. **Gated Delta Networks** - Yang, Kautz, Hatamizadeh; Dec 2024; arXiv:2412.06464. The backbone. Delta rule + gating improves retrieval over Mamba2. ICE relation: ICE is added on top.
8. **Log-Linear Attention** - Guo, Yang, Goel, Xing, Dao, Kim; Jun 2025; arXiv:2506.04761 (ICLR 2026). State grows O(log T) via Fenwick tree; log-linear Mamba2 / GDN keep recall at longer lengths. ICE relation: a state-expansion competitor at O(log T) memory; ICE is O(T) but with 41 B/token; compare bytes/token curves. Combine: log-linear GDN + ICE.
9. **MesaNet** - von Oswald et al.; Jun 2025; arXiv:2506.05233 (ICLR 2026). Locally-optimal test-time regression via conjugate gradient; better long-context than DeltaNet at higher inference compute. ICE relation: a stronger fixed-state baseline; ICE is cheaper at inference.
10. **Titans** - Behrouz, Zhong, Mirrokni; Dec 2024; arXiv:2501.00663. Neural long-term memory updated at test time + attention for the short term. **ATLAS** - Behrouz et al.; May 2025; arXiv:2505.23735. Optimises memory over a window of tokens, ~80% on BABILong at 10M. ICE relation: learned compressive memory vs exact symbolic memory; complementary (ICE for verbatim spans, test-time memory for gist).
11. **PaTH Attention** - Yang, Shen, Wen, Tan, Mishra, Ren, Panda, Kim; May 2025; arXiv:2505.16381. Data-dependent Householder position encoding for softmax attention; better on state-tracking/recall synthetics. ICE relation: improves the attention layers of the hybrid; orthogonal, low priority.
12. **Kimi Linear (KDA)** - Kimi Team; Oct 2025; arXiv:2510.26692. Finer-grained-gated DeltaNet + MLA hybrid (3:1), cuts KV 75%. ICE relation: shows production hybrids still keep full attention; ICE variant of "1:15 + ICE" tests if attention can be reduced further.
13. **LoLA: Low-Rank Linear Attention with Sparse Caching** - McDermott, Heath, Parhi; May 2025 (v3 Jun 2026); arXiv:2505.23666. Training-free: sliding window + small sparse global KV cache holding pairs that the linear state fails to recall ("self-recall error") + recurrent state; passkey 0.6% -> 97.4% with 4.6x smaller cache. ICE relation: the most direct "cheap sparse cache" competitor. It keeps KV for few selected hard pairs; ICE keeps token IDs for all positions. Compare at matched bytes/token; possible combo: ICE decides what is "easy" (exact repeat) so the sparse KV budget is spent elsewhere.

## C. Hybrid design (which attention is needed)
14. **A Systematic Analysis of Hybrid Linear Attention** - Wang et al.; Jul 2025 (v2 Jun 2026); arXiv:2507.06457. 72 models; recall rises with # full attn layers, steeply below 3:1; recommend GDN/HGRN-2 at 3:1-6:1. ICE relation: justifies arm A (3:1) vs B (7:1) vs F (15:1) design; ICE claim "F beats/equals A" would be a clean result.
15. **Rethinking the Role of Efficient Attention in Hybrid Architectures** - Qiao, Xu, Xiao, Su, Zhou, Chen, Xu, Han, Liu; Jun 2026; arXiv:2606.15378. Full attention carries nearly all long-range retrieval; "Large-Window Laziness" (big SWA windows delay retrieval-head formation); NoPE in full layers helps long context. ICE relation: ICE may similarly make attention layers "lazy" (matches the over-reliance observed: C_off worse than B). Test: does C's attention layer still form retrieval/induction heads? NoPE-in-attention-layers is a cheap ablation.
16. **HydraHead** - Tan, Chen, Shen, Liu, Shen, Wu, Ye; Jun 2026; arXiv:2606.20097. Head-level hybrid: keep full attention only for retrieval-critical heads; 7:1 LA:FA matches 3:1 layer hybrid; 15B-token distillation. ICE relation: head-level selection of where attention is needed; ICE could replace some of those retrieval heads; compare "ICE vs HydraHead at 7:1".
17. **The Token Before the Value Is the Key: How Hybrid Architectures Organize Induction Circuits** - Cheng et al.; Sep 2026; arXiv:2609.15545. In hybrids, "carrying" (previous-token info) sits in efficient layers, "matching" in global layers. ICE relation: ICE hands the model the match + successor directly, which should make the matching circuit redundant; a probing study on arm C vs B is a cheap mechanistic figure, and a natural explanation of over-reliance.
18. **Tail-Replay** - Liu, Qi, Wu, Liu, Chen; Aug 2026; arXiv:2608.30310. Prefix caching for GDN hybrids: cache full-attn KV, rebuild linear state by replaying a short suffix (5-10% budget, 92.8-99.9% quality). ICE relation: confirms GDN state is a lossy, recency-weighted compression of the prefix (supports ICE's premise); and ICE's table is cheap to prefix-cache (token IDs).
19. Production hybrids: **Samba** (arXiv:2406.07522, Ren et al. 2024, Mamba + SWA), **Hymba** (arXiv:2411.13676, Dong et al. 2024, parallel attn+SSM heads, meta tokens), **Nemotron-H** (arXiv:2504.03624, NVIDIA 2025, mostly Mamba, few attn layers), Jamba (arXiv:2403.19887 [not opened]), Zamba (arXiv:2405.16712 [not opened]). Qwen3-Next / MiniMax: no arXiv opened. ICE relation: ICE is a drop-in for any of them; argue it matters most for the low-attention-ratio ones (Nemotron-H).

## D. Sparse / selective attention as cheap recall
20. **Retrieval Head Mechanistically Explains Long-Context Factuality** - Wu, Wang, Xiao, Peng, Fu; Apr 2024; arXiv:2404.15574. <5% of heads do retrieval, causally necessary. ICE relation: ICE could be framed as "externalising the retrieval heads' copy function".
21. **DuoAttention** - Xiao, Tang, Zuo, Guo, Yang, Tang, Fu, Han; Oct 2024; arXiv:2410.10819. Full KV only for retrieval heads, constant KV for streaming heads; 2.55x memory cut. ICE relation: baseline for "KV-light attention" memory; ICE reduces the retrieval heads' work further.
22. **Native Sparse Attention (NSA)** - Yuan et al.; Feb 2025; arXiv:2502.11089. Trainable compress + select + window sparse attention. **MoBA** - Lu et al.; Feb 2025; arXiv:2502.13189. MoE-style block selection. **Quest** - Tang et al.; Jun 2024; arXiv:2406.10774. Query-aware page selection with min/max key summaries. **TokenSelect** - Wu et al.; Nov 2024; arXiv:2411.02886. Per-token KV selection by q.k. ICE relation: all select KV by *learned similarity* and still store the full KV; none uses an exact token-ID index. ICE's index is 50x smaller but cannot do semantic matches.
23. **MATCH: Modulating Attention via In-Context Retrieval** - Ma et al.; Jun 2026; arXiv:2606.29844 (ACL 2026). Sparse-attention transformers (SWA layers) + external retriever (Sentence-BERT + reranker; exact string match on MQAR/MAD) that unmasks retrieved positions; recomputes Q/K/V of retrieved text; needs continual training; base is a transformer, not linear/hybrid. ICE relation: **closest "lookup-guided attention" prior art found** but retrieval is semantic/chunk-level and the base is SWA transformer. Novelty risk for the "match -> route sparse attention" variant (see below), minor for ICE itself.
24. Landmark attention, InfLLM, RetrievalAttention, MagicPIG [not opened]: not verified; mention only as "KV retrieval via similarity", do not cite without checking.

## E. Exact-match / n-gram / pointer prior art (novelty risk for ICE core)
25. **Engram: Conditional Memory via Scalable Lookup** - Cheng, Tian, Zeng, Dai, ..., Liang (DeepSeek/PKU); Jan 2026 (v2 Jul 2026); arXiv:2601.07372. Static hashed N-gram embedding tables injected in early layers (O(1) lookup, host-memory prefetch), 27B scale; reports better MQ-NIAH (84.2 -> 97.0) by offloading local dependencies. ICE relation: **name collision and architectural cousin** ("In-Context Engram"). Difference: Engram looks up *learned static embeddings* keyed by n-gram hashes (parametric knowledge); ICE looks up *the in-context successor token* (non-parametric, per-document). Must be cited and the distinction stated first on the poster. I only read the abstract page; details of its gating are not verified.
26. **Infini-gram** - Liu, Min, Zettlemoyer, Choi, Hajishirzi; Jan 2024; arXiv:2401.17377. Suffix-array infinity-gram LM over trillions of tokens; predicts next token 47% right (matches ICE Day-0 46.7% coincidentally); interpolating with neural LMs reduces perplexity. ICE relation: ICE = in-document infini-gram, trained into the net. Their "agreement vs suffix length" analysis parallels ICE's trust signals.
27. **Improving Neural LMs with a Continuous Cache** (Grave, Joulin, Usunier; arXiv:1612.04426) and **Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models** (Merity, Xiong, Bradbury, Socher; arXiv:1609.07843); **kNN-LM** and gated variants (Drozdov et al. 2022: adapt interpolation weight to retrieval quality, ~4% ppl). ICE relation: ICE output-side is a learned cache/pointer; the "trust" idea has precedent in adaptive lambda; novelty should rest on hybrid-recurrent + CPU table + bytes/token, not on the idea of a cache.
28. **Prompt Lookup Decoding** (llama.cpp / apoorvumang; no arXiv) and **REST** (He, Zhong, Cai, Lee, He; Nov 2023; arXiv:2311.08252): n-gram/suffix match proposes draft tokens at inference, verified by target. No work found that *trains* the model on matches. ICE relation: (a) synergy: ICE's longest-match successor is a free draft token for speculative decoding (use ICE as the draft, verified by model; accept rate likely high on code); (b) ICE trains on matches - the gap.

## F. Most promising directions for an ICE variant
1. **Lookup-guided sparse attention ("ICE-Attn")**: use the n-gram table's *positions* (not just the successor token) to select 1-8 earlier positions; run a tiny sparse attention in one layer over those positions' hidden states (or over the hidden state at p, the position BEFORE the successor, which holds the "carried" context per 2609.15545). This fixes the stated ICE failures: wrong-object value (conflict_g5) and paraphrase beyond span, because the model reads context around the match and can reject it. Cost: stores hidden states for matched positions only (needs KV for those, or recompute from token IDs as MATCH does) - weakens the bytes/token claim; report a budgeted version (e.g., store layer-k hidden for a 1-in-16 subsample or recompute a 32-token window by re-running the early layers). Novelty: MATCH is the nearest but uses semantic retrieval on transformers; moderate novelty risk, so frame as "exact-index routing for hybrids".
2. **Candidate-set, not single successor**: return top-k successors (all distinct continuations seen for the longest matching n-gram with counts and recency) as a set into the input/output side, so conflicts (the trust failure) are visible to the model; trust signals failed because agree/rel are summaries of a single candidate. Cheap to implement in the data loader; clean ablation vs C. Low novelty risk (infini-gram-style counts), high chance of fixing "follows wrong lookup".
3. **ICE as speculative draft + calibration**: use ICE longest-match as draft tokens for speculative decoding of the hybrid; reports real speed along with memory (reviewer item 3). Novelty: training with matches + verifying is open; PLD/REST are training-free.
4. **Mechanistic story + anti-laziness**: probe whether attention layers in C lose induction heads (2606.15378, 2609.15545 predict this); test F (15:1) and C with NoPE attention layers or higher ICE-dropout; if C_off < B is due to laziness, schedule dropout (curriculum) or an auxiliary loss with ICE masked. Cheap analysis, strong faculty-friendly narrative.
5. **Combine with state-expansion/recall-aware hybrids** (Log-Linear GDN, LoLA-style sparse cache) for a Pareto plot (bytes/token vs recall) with BASED-style axes; ICE alone cannot beat LoLA on semantic recall, so claim only the exact-match regime and show the complementary combo.

## G. Novelty risks
- Engram (2601.07372): name + "n-gram lookup injected in early layers" overlap; state clearly that ICE is non-parametric in-context successor lookup. Highest risk reviewer comparison.
- Akyurek et al. 2024 (2401.12973): hard-wired n-gram heads in non-attention LMs give perplexity gains. The "exact n-gram helps recurrent models" idea is not new; ICE's new parts are the O(1)-memory table, trust buckets, hybrid GDN setting.
- Infini-gram / continuous cache / pointer sentinel / kNN-LM: classical "cache + gate" family. Avoid claiming the cache idea as novel.
- MATCH (2606.29844): if ICE adds match-routed attention, overlap on "exact string matching routes sparse attention".
- No unverified claims: I did not open Jamba, Zamba, Qwen3-Next, MiniMax, Landmark, InfLLM; do not cite without checking.
